Our Approach

100% hands-on. Zero slides.

AI is not something you can learn by listening. You have to use it, break things, and build intuition through practice. That's exactly what our sessions are.

Your code, your projects

We don't use contrived examples. You bring your actual work — the codebase you're building, the problem you're stuck on — and we work through it together in real time.

You drive, we navigate

You'll have your hands on the keyboard. We pair-program with you, showing techniques in the context of your own projects so the learning sticks immediately.

Learn by doing, not watching

Every session ends with something you built or improved. Not notes from a lecture — actual working code, a new workflow, or a problem solved.

Mistakes are the curriculum

The best way to learn what AI tools can and can't do is to try things that don't work. We create a safe space to experiment, fail fast, and build real understanding.
